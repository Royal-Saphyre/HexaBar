HexBar

Hexagon-styled action bars for World of Warcraft 3.3.5, built to keep your bars in their original Blizzard screen positions — just smaller, tighter, and reshaped into a honeycomb layout with a gold gryphon endcap on the main bar.

Includes a Quick Bind system: an extra button in the game menu (Esc) that lets you rebind action bar keys on the fly, with modifier support and overwrite warnings, without ever opening the full keybindings UI.
